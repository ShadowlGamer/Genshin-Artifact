How to Build Characters

Know Your Character's Role

A character can perform one of three roles: Main DPS, Sub DPS, or Support. Knowing their role will help you to appropriately equip them with the right weapons, artifacts, and teammates.

Checking the character's talent and kit can help you figure out what their role and capabilities are.

Role Explanation
Main DPS The main DPS is the primary source of damage for the party. They are expected to appear on the field more frequently than their allies and output significant damage.
Sub DPS The Sub DPS's role is to provide additional damage through their Elemental Skills or Bursts, either On or Off-Field. The Sub-DPS steps in to take over for the Main DPS during downtimes in rotation, ensuring that damage remains constant.
Support The Support's role is to heal or buff allies. Supports deal low damage but compensate by increasing the survivability of your party.

Know Your Character Teams

Additionally, creating specific builds for characters requires you to know which teammates they will be operating with. Being placed in teams with buffers like Furina or Bennett may change your character's build.

On that note, do your best to research their teammates and what they will bring for your character, as that can drastically change what weapons, artifacts, and stats you will want to prioritize.

Equip Suitable Artifacts

After determining the character role, equip them with an artifact that will increase their combat effectiveness. Artifacts are equipment that can boost stats and damage. When combined, each artifact can provide a variety of additional effects; find the ones that benefit your character the most.

Know What Stats to Prioritize

When equipping artifacts other than the set effects, it is critical to select those with stats that will benefit your character. Each character will scale based on a specific stat, so make sure you understand that by examining their talents to make your character more effective.
List of Artifact Sets and Stats

Balance Your Substats

Prioritizing substats does not often mean neglecting the rest of their stats. Most DPS characters, for instance, can benefit from small doses of Energy Recharge, and some Swirl units still enjoy trace amounts of ATK%.

If all substats on your artifacts are of equal upgrade strength, then consider prioritizing artifacts with more beneficial and well-rounded substats.

Check Weapon Skill and Substats


Check the weapon's skill and substat to see if it suits the character's role and playstyle. You will want to equip a weapon that, depending on their role, will improve your character even further.

For example, Main DPS characters will want a weapon that can increase their Attack DMG and Skill DMG, while Sub DPS will desire weapons that improve the effectiveness of Elemental Skills and Bursts, whether by increasing damage or decreasing cooldowns.

And for Supports, you'll want weapons with Energy Recharge and Buff Effects to boost their supporting abilities.

Equip For Necessity

Sometimes slotting a supposed damage-increasing weapon for your character is not enough. Ask yourself if your character needs the damage or the utility that the weapon provides.

This is especially true for Supports like Kazuha and Sucrose, who need both Elemental Mastery and Energy Recharge to operate at peak performance.

In this case, if their Elemental Mastery needs are met, giving them weapons like Favonius Sword or Favonius Codex respectively will do more for their overall effectiveness.
